Where did the 10,000 tokens go?

One shared vocabulary: a base alphabet (built from the full corpus, guarantees zero UNK) plus two priority phases of merges.

1,495 base symbols — full corpus + punctuation/whitespace
2,389 Phase 1 — forcing English below 1.2
6,116 Phase 2 — equalizing all four languages

The arithmetic

sorted tokens-per-word ratios
  X_min  English   1513 tokens / 1300 words  = 1.16385
         Hindi     1513 tokens / 1300 words  = 1.16385
         Telugu    1513 tokens / 1300 words  = 1.16385
  X_max  Spanish   1514 tokens / 1300 words  = 1.16462

  gap  = X_max − X_min
       = 1.16462 − 1.16385
       = 0.00077

  score = 1000 ÷ gap
        = 1000 ÷ 0.00077
